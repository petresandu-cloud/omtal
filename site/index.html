Seen by the machines that answer.

Omtal reads your site the way a crawler does, derives the questions your product answers, asks the engines, and says on every finding what was found, why it matters and how it knows.

Reads up to 12 pages, takes under a minute, and gives you the same report the command line writes. Nothing is stored but the report, which is kept for an hour at a link you can share. No engine is asked here; for that, run Omtal with your own key.

See a sample report Install from GitHub

pip install git+https://github.com/petresandu-cloud/omtal
omtal audit https://your-product.example      # writes omtal-out/<host>/report.html

The ladder

  • unreachable the machines that answer questions cannot read the site
  • reachable they can read it, but what the product is has to be guessed
  • understood the product is declared in a form machines read
  • cited engines use the site as a source when answering
  • recommended engines name the product for the questions it answers
Reads what the engines readrobots policy per crawler, sitemap, llms.txt, and every page's title, description, canonical, headings, structured data and text.
Asks, then countsWith your key it asks Brave, OpenAI or Anthropic the questions your product answers, stores every answer, and speaks in fractions: mentioned, cited, recommended.
Says how it knowsEvery finding carries its provenance and why it matters. No composite score, no page generation, no promises about ranking.
