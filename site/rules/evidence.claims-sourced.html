Claims with numbers are backed by a source or a method

The question For each claim with a number on the site (the list is in the product graph), is there a source, a method or a link that substantiates it on the same page? Name the claim and where the evidence is, or say it is unsupported.

Why it matters

Engines increasingly weigh evidence: a number with a source is citable, a number alone is marketing.
