# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-08

First release.

- Reads a site the way a crawler does: robots policy per crawler, sitemaps,
  llms.txt, a bounded crawl with title, description, canonical, headings,
  structured data, text and links per page.
- A product graph with declared and apparent halves kept apart.
- Questions derived from the declared capabilities; a person or model may
  add or strike them, with provenance.
- An observatory over documented engine APIs (Brave Search, OpenAI with web
  search, Anthropic with web search), keys from the environment, every answer
  stored verbatim and counted in fractions.
- 24 rules in four layers, two of them judgement rules; a ladder from
  unreachable to recommended; a report with a fixed contract, one colour per
  status, exports carried inside the page, and a fingerprint that catches
  hand edits.
