## The name

**Omtal.** Two syllables, said "OM-tahl". Swedish: mention, talk about; and,
in *gott omtal*, a good name. That is the whole product in one word: does the
web talk about your product, and do the machines that answer questions
mention it well? Editerra AB is a Swedish company, and the sibling product,
Okkok, carries the same accent.

Write it *Omtal* in prose and *omtal* in the wordmark and in commands. Never
OMTAL, never Om-Tal.

## The line

**Seen by the machines that answer.**

Shorter, under the wordmark: **AI discoverability check.** That is also the
title every report carries.

## The mark

A rounded square, the shape of an app icon, holding an eye: the lens open,
the pupil mint. To be seen is the point; the square is the family
resemblance to Okkok's gate. Colour, one-colour and logo versions are in
`assets/`. Keep it whole; minimum 16 pixels; the mono version reads better
small.

## Colour

| Role | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Indigo | `#3A2E7A` | the brand ground, the accent in the report |
| Mint | `#9BE7C4` | the pupil, and nothing else; shared with Okkok's check |
| Ink | `#141414` | text |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | ground |

The status colours in the report (red, amber, blue, grey, green) are semantic,
fixed by `REPORT-PRINCIPLES.md`, and not brand colours.

## Voice

Findings, not opinions. What was found, why it matters, how we know. No
promises of ranking: the engines decide, Omtal measures.

## What is commercial

- **Omtal Watch.** The engines asked on your behalf, repeatedly, with the
  observations kept as a longitudinal record: when a change on your site
  moved an engine, and when a competitor moved in.
- **Signed reports** produced through Editerra's service.
- **A commercial licence** for building the engine into a closed product or
  a hosted service, and support under an agreement.
